## Import preparation

`node scripts/import-adaptive-catalog.mjs` validates the local artifact and previews approved products without connecting to Supabase. Once every record has an evidence-backed approval reference and `approved_fields` covers every imported field, and current facts have been independently checked, `node scripts/import-adaptive-catalog.mjs --apply` writes approved records only. The process must receive `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL` (or `SUPABASE_URL`) for the confirmed project and a separately authorized server-side `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` in its environment. No service key was found in the inspected environment or `.env`. Never expose it in a browser or commit it. Setting the service role is not a substitute for rights approval.

The importer writes products, offers, seller evidence, and linked feature attributes. IDs are deterministic and upserts use those IDs. It refuses a conflicting existing product URL and reads each written row back. Prices, material fields, variant data, images, and shipping are intentionally not written until a separately reviewed mapping exists. It is not transactional across tables or records; a failed write can leave partial rows and requires investigation before a retry. Removing or changing a previously imported feature does not remove older rows; this importer is a small-batch prototype, not a complete synchronization or takedown system. Check for duplicate offers under alternate URLs and resolve identity conflicts manually; a product left without its offer after an interrupted import is treated as a conflict. No live write, idempotent rerun, or remote readback was possible with the current approval and credential state.
